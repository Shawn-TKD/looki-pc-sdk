# Repository contents

This repository contains independently written interoperability code, protocol descriptions,
analysis tools, and small factual metadata extracted from an official update package. It does not
contain flashable Looki firmware, APK files, partition images, native libraries, media, raw
Bluetooth captures, account tokens, device credentials, or hotspot passwords.

Files under `firmware/` are provided for research identification and reproducibility. They do not
grant a license to vendor software or trademarks.

Looki and related names may be trademarks of their respective owners. This project is unofficial
and is not endorsed by or affiliated with the device vendor.
